Questions

Do I have to use the pool checklist?

No. The defaults are pool leak detection because that's who we built it for, but every section, item and heading is editable. Plenty of it works unchanged for equipment-only or plumbing calls.

What happens to my reports if I stop paying?

You keep read access and can export every report to PDF. Nothing is deleted without you asking.

Can customers see each other's reports?

No. A report is only visible to the exact email address you shared it with, and that's enforced by the database rather than the app. Customers of different companies never see each other at all.

Does it work without signal at the pool?

Not yet — saving a report needs a connection. If you're regularly out of range, write the report up in the truck once you're back on signal.

Can I move my existing template across?

Yes. Build it once under Preset Sections and every new report starts from it. Send us your current form and we'll set it up for you.
